# Press kit

## Name

Claude Notch. Not made by or affiliated with Anthropic; "Claude" is Anthropic's.

## One line

Every Claude account open at once, with each one's limits beside the MacBook notch.

## Two lines

Claude Notch runs the Claude desktop app once per account, each signed in on its own, and shows every account's 5-hour and weekly limits beside the MacBook notch. Free, for macOS 26.

## Facts

- Made by Doug Silkstone.
- For macOS 26 or later and the Claude desktop app. Works on screens without a notch.
- Free; source on GitHub: https://github.com/dougwithseismic/claude-multi-account
- Site: https://dougwithseismic.github.io/claude-multi-account/
- What it reads: each copy's stored login, read-only. What it sends: the usage and profile checks Claude Code's /usage makes, every 5 minutes per account.
