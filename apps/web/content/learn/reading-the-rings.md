Claude Notch draws each copy of Claude as a double ring beside the notch, and opens into a panel with a bar for each limit. Drag the sliders to see what each part does.

<div data-figure="rings"></div>

## The outer ring: five hours

The outer ring fills as you use the 5-hour window. At 90% it turns red, so you notice before you run out. At 100% it stays red and empties as the wait runs down, so a glance tells you roughly how long until you're back.

## The inner ring: the week

The inner ring is the fullest weekly limit, whether that's all models or one model's own limit, like Fable's. When a weekly limit is full, the inner ring becomes a red cross: that account is out until its week resets, however much of the 5-hour window is left.

## The bars and the tick

The panel has a bar for each window, with its percent and when it resets. The white tick on each bar marks how far through the window you are, so fill past the tick means you're using it faster than an even pace. [Pacing](/learn/pacing) explains the arithmetic.

## Everything else

- A dot under a ring marks the copy in front.
- A faded ring has old numbers: that copy's login expired while it was closed, and opening it fixes that.
- When every copy is out, the closed notch counts down to the first one back.

[Reading the panel](/docs/panel) lists every message the panel can show.
